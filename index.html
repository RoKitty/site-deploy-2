  • Right in your browser

    RoKitty is a web extension. Just install it in your browser and you're ready to go.

  • Not a theme or patch

    Other extensions just modify Roblox.com. RoKitty is entirely separate, and you can swap back and forth anytime.

  • Completely local

    RoKitty is a custom client that lives on your computer. Your Roblox credentials never get sent away.

    RoKitty doesn't just reimplement Roblox.com, it adds tons of features that make Roblox just plain better to use.
